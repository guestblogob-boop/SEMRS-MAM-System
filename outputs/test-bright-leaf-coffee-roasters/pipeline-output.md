## 1. Research Agent

**Audience insights:**
- Local buyers care most about freshness and provenance — "roasted
  this week," origin story of the beans — over generic quality claims.
- Community connection matters: customers who discover independent
  cafés through neighborhood events and word of mouth convert better
  than cold ad traffic (relevant since this client isn't running ads).
- Online specialty-coffee buyers increasingly choose subscriptions over
  one-off purchases — convenience and "never run out" beat price
  sensitivity for this segment.
- Repeat café customers respond to seeing the roasting process, not
  just the finished cup — behind-the-scenes content performs well in
  this category.
- Gift-giving is a meaningful secondary motivator for bean subscriptions
  (holidays, birthdays) — worth at least one content angle.

**Trends / competitor moves:**
- Specialty coffee subscriptions are a growing category nationally;
  competitors emphasize single-origin storytelling over blends.
- Several comparable local roasters partner with neighborhood events
  (farmers markets, pop-ups) and post about it — a low-cost visibility
  tactic that fits a client not running ads.

**Channels this audience actually uses most:**
Instagram (visual, food/drink discovery), Facebook (local community
groups/events), Email (existing loyal customers already on the list).

**Handoff to SEO & GEO Agent:** research summary above.
