## 1. Research Agent

**Audience insights:**
- Local dental search is overwhelmingly near-me/local-intent ("dentist
  near me," "[city] dentist," "emergency dentist [city]").
- Dental anxiety is a real decision factor — patients look for
  reassurance signals (welcoming office photos, gentle language, clear
  what-to-expect info) before booking, directly relevant to BrightLeaf's
  "warm, professional, reassuring" tone.
- Online reviews (Google Business Profile, Facebook) are a primary
  trust signal for choosing a local dental provider.
- Booking friction matters — patients abandon if a clear "Book Now"
  path or phone number isn't immediately visible.
- Younger/cosmetic-interested patients research via visual content
  (Instagram) more than search alone; general appointment-seekers lean
  on search + website.

**Trends / competitor moves:**
- Local dental practices increasingly publish patient-education blog
  content ("what to expect at your first visit," procedure FAQs).
- Competitor practices commonly run "meet the team"/testimonial content
  on Instagram/Facebook.
- Active Google Business Profile use is common among local dental
  competitors — noted as an observed pattern only, since GBP isn't in
  BrightLeaf's stated channel scope.

**Which in-scope channels this audience actually uses most:**
Website/Blog (primary), Facebook (high), Instagram (moderate — younger/
cosmetic patients), WhatsApp (post-booking, not discovery), Email
(existing-patient retention, not discovery), LinkedIn (low relevance —
flagged honestly rather than inflated).

**Handoff to SEO & GEO Agent:** research summary above.
