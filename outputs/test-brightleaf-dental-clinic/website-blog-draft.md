Booking a first appointment with a new dentist can feel like a bigger
decision than it should be. If you've put it off because you're not
sure what the visit actually involves — or because dental visits make
you a little uneasy — you're not alone, and it's exactly why we
structured our first-visit process the way we did.

**H2: Before You Arrive** — You'll fill out a short intake form
covering your dental history and any concerns you want to flag —
including anxiety. Tell us up front if you'd like a slower pace or
extra explanation at each step; we'd rather know than guess.

**H2: The Exam** — A gentle, thorough look at your teeth and gums, plus
X-rays if needed. We'll walk you through what we're seeing in plain
language, not jargon.

**H2: The Conversation** — Before any cleaning or treatment, we talk
through what we found and what (if anything) we'd recommend next — no
surprise procedures, no pressure.

**H2: Booking Your Next Step** — If follow-up care is needed, we'll
help you schedule it before you leave, so you're not left chasing a
callback.

If dental visits have made you anxious in the past, tell us. A calm,
unhurried first visit is the whole point.
