## Research Summary
Local dental search is overwhelmingly near-me/local-intent. Dental
anxiety is a real booking barrier — patients look for reassurance
signals before booking. Online reviews are the primary trust signal.
Booking friction (no visible "Book Now" path) causes drop-off. Most-
used in-scope channels: Website/Blog (primary), Facebook (high),
Instagram (moderate), WhatsApp/Email (retention, not discovery),
LinkedIn (low relevance, flagged honestly). Full detail:
outputs/test-brightleaf-dental-clinic/pipeline-output.md, section 1.
