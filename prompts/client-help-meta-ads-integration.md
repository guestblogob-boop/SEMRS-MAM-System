# Client Help: How to Give SEMRS Access to Your Meta Ad Account (3 Minutes)

This is what the client sees in SEMRS OS → Ads Campaigns →
Client Help, once an ads-scoped order has passed CEO Budget & Campaign
Approval (see agents/ads-agent.md, Process step 8, which this guide
fulfills). The auto-verification step described below ("Access
Verified ✅") is target dashboard functionality, not yet built — see
CLAUDE.md, Client Contact Channel, "Intended dashboard behavior," for
the same not-yet-built framing applied elsewhere in this system. Until
it exists, a human at SEMRS confirms access manually in Meta Business
Manager.

**You will never share your Facebook password. We only need official
agency access via Business ID.**

The $1,000/month budget used throughout this guide is a worked example
for illustration — the real number is whatever was actually agreed in
this client's Budget & Campaign Approval, not a default or minimum.

## What You Need Before Starting
- A Facebook account (personal)
- Your Business Page (e.g., ABC Company)
- A credit/debit card for Meta to charge your ad budget

## Part A: If you already have Business Manager (most clients)

**Step 1 — Go to Business Settings**
1. Open business.facebook.com
2. Top left: select your Business (or create one)
3. Click Business Settings (gear icon)

**Step 2 — Add SEMRS as a Partner**
1. Left menu: click Partners
2. Click Add → "Give a partner access to your assets"
3. It will ask for Partner Business ID
4. Ask your SEMRS account manager for our current Business ID and enter it here
5. Click Next

**Prefer not to look anything up?** Skip to Part C below — SEMRS can send
you the request instead, and you just approve it.

**Step 3 — Select what to share**
- Ad Accounts: select your Ad Account → give access: Manage Campaigns,
  Manage Ad Account, View Performance
- Pages: select your Facebook Page → give access: Manage Page, Create
  Ads
- Instagram Account: select your Instagram (if connected) → Manage
- Pixel (if you have one): select Pixel → Manage
- Click Save Changes

**Step 4 — Done in Meta.** You'll see SEMRS listed as a partner with
"Pending" or "Active."

**Step 5 — Come back to SEMRS OS**
1. Go to SEMRS OS → My Campaigns → Ads Campaigns
2. Click "I Have Granted Access — Verify Now"
3. The system checks within ~2 minutes and shows "Access Verified ✅"

## Part B: If you don't have Business Manager / an Ad Account yet (first time)

**Step 1 — Create Business Manager**
1. Go to business.facebook.com/overview
2. Click Create Business
3. Enter your business name, your name, business email → Submit

**Step 2 — Create Ad Account**
1. Inside Business Settings → left menu: Accounts → Ad Accounts
2. Click Add → Create a New Ad Account
3. Name it (e.g. "ABC Company Ads"), set your timezone and currency
4. Add yourself as admin

**Step 3 — Add a payment method (for your ad budget)**
1. Left menu: Accounts → Ad Accounts → select your Ad Account
2. Go to Billing → Payment Settings → Add Payment Method
3. Add your credit/debit card — Meta bills this card directly, in
   daily increments, until your agreed monthly budget is spent

**Step 4 — Now give SEMRS access.** Follow Part A, Steps 1–5, above.

## Part C: Ask SEMRS to Request Access Instead (no ID to look up)

Prefer not to hunt down a Business ID yourself? SEMRS can send you the
request instead — you just approve it.

1. Tell your SEMRS account manager your Business name or Facebook Page
   name/ID (found on your Page's About section, or in Business
   Settings).
2. SEMRS staff opens our own Meta Business Settings → Partners → "Ask a
   partner to assign assets," and sends the request to your Business/
   Page using what you gave us.
3. You'll see the incoming request in your own Business Settings →
   Requests — approve it there, choosing exactly which assets (Ad
   Account, Page, Instagram, Pixel) to share.
4. Done — same result as Part A, just no ID for you to type in.
5. Remove SEMRS's access anytime: Business Settings → Partners →
   Remove — same as Part A.

## After You Grant Access — What Happens Next?
1. You keep control of your budget. The money stays in **your** Meta
   account — Meta bills **your** card, never SEMRS.
2. SEMRS runs campaigns inside your Ad Account using our agency access
   — we never move the money ourselves.
3. You pay SEMRS only our management fee (15% of your Meta ad spend,
   $30/month minimum — see CLAUDE.md, Paid Media Model, for how this
   is calculated for your specific budget). If you also run ads on
   another platform through SEMRS, that platform's fee is calculated
   the same way, separately, and added — never one blended rate on
   your combined spend. We send an invoice via SEMRS OS's
   Invoice section — never deducted from your ad spend.
4. Meta charges your card in smaller daily increments until your
   agreed monthly budget is reached.

## Important Security Notes
- **Never** share your Facebook password with SEMRS or anyone else.
- We only ever need Partner Access via Business ID — nothing more.
- You can remove SEMRS's access anytime: Business Settings → Partners
  → Remove.
- You own your Ad Account, Page, and all data — forever, not just for
  the length of the engagement.
